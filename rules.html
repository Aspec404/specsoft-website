Server & Website Rules

Rules for everyone

These apply everywhere: the website, chats, communities, our Discord server and our Roblox community.

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, threats or targeting people.
  2. No hate. No slurs, discrimination or hateful content about who someone is.
  3. Keep it safe. No explicit, graphic or gory content. Anything involving minors in a sexual or harmful way is banned immediately and reported.
  4. Protect privacy. Do not share anyone’s personal information (doxxing) or private messages without permission.
  5. No spam or bots. No flooding, mass messages, fake accounts, or scripts that spam sign-up, login or chats.
  6. Be real. Do not impersonate other people or pretend to be staff, and do not fake badges or roles.
  7. No scams or malware. No phishing, token grabbers, suspicious links or anything meant to harm devices or accounts.
  8. No ban evasion. Making a new account to get around a ban or suspension is not allowed.
  9. Follow the law and each platform’s rules, including the Discord and Roblox terms of service.

Website rules

  • Profiles: usernames, display names, bios, avatars and themes must follow these rules. No offensive names or inappropriate pictures.
  • Projects: only publish projects you made or have permission to share. Give credit where it is due.
  • Comments: keep them on topic and kind. Constructive feedback is welcome.
  • Custom text: your nickname and your custom typing indicator are seen by others, so they follow the same rules.
  • Store: do not cheat, exploit bugs or abuse the system to get Credits or items. Report bugs to us instead.
  • Security: do not try to break into the site, admin tools, accounts or database. Report any weakness you find to us privately.

Chat and community rules

  • Stay on topic in each channel and use the right place for your message.
  • No advertising or self-promotion without permission from the community’s moderators.
  • Do not send images or files that are explicit, graphic or harmful. Images that look suggestive may be blurred for other people.
  • Do not try to hide harsh words with odd spelling or symbols to get around the sensitive-message filter.
  • Respect moderators. If you disagree with a decision, appeal it instead of arguing in the chat.
  • Community owners can add extra rules, as long as they do not conflict with these.

Discord server and Roblox

  • Everything above applies in our Discord server and in our Roblox community.
  • No cheating, exploiting or sharing exploit tools for Roblox games.
  • Follow staff instructions and keep voice chat friendly.

Reporting

See something that breaks the rules? Use the Report option on a message, or tell a moderator in our Discord server. Please do not retaliate or take matters into your own hands. False or spam reports may be treated as rule breaking.

What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is and what happened before, staff may:

  • remove the content or give a warning;
  • restrict your posting or commenting for a while;
  • suspend your account for a set time;
  • permanently ban your account.

Serious breaking of the rules can skip straight to a ban.

Appeals

If you think a decision was a mistake, you can appeal through our Discord server or the appeal option on the ban screen. Please include your username and what happened. We will review it and tell you the result.

About these rules

These rules may change as the community grows. Staff make the final call on what counts as breaking them. See also our Terms & Guidelines and Privacy Policy.
