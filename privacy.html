Who we are

SPECSOFT® (“we”, “us”) is an independent software and game development brand. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, your account, chats, profiles, the Store and other community features, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Our Discord server and Roblox group are run on platforms owned by other companies. This policy does not cover those platforms; their own policies apply there.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username, display name and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and we never see or store them in plain text.
  • Profile content: your avatar, bio, theme, links, projects, highlights, followers and following, likes, reposts and comments.
  • Chat content: messages, replies, reactions, images and files you send, pinned messages, nicknames, mute and block choices, group or community details, and reports you file.
  • Activity and presence: whether you are online, the “online now” counter, and live typing signals. Typing signals are short-lived and are not saved.
  • Settings: your theme, effects, cursor and chat options, and your custom typing indicator text.
  • Store data: your APEX Credits balance and the items you own.
  • Moderation records: reports, warnings, restrictions, suspensions and bans, including the reason, moderator notes, evidence IDs and review dates.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These appear in server logs kept by our hosting and database providers.

How we use it

  • To run the site: sign you in, show your profile, deliver chats and keep your settings.
  • To keep the community safe: handle reports, stop spam and bots, and enforce our Rules and Terms, including bans.
  • To make the site work well: fix bugs, prevent abuse and improve features.
  • To tell you things you need to know, such as notifications inside the site.

We do not run ads on this site.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see what

  • Public: the parts of your profile you publish, such as your username, display name, avatar, projects and comments.
  • Chat members: messages and files you send in a chat are visible to the people in that chat. Owners and moderators of a community can see that community’s content.
  • Staff: SPECSOFT staff with admin access may view content and account records when they investigate a report or enforce our rules.
  • Only you: your settings, your email address and your Store balance. Your email is not shown on your public profile.

Your custom typing indicator text is shown to other people in the chat while you type, so it follows our Rules.

When new privacy controls are added to Settings (for example private accounts or who can see your profile), they will be described here.

Image safety check

To blur suggestive or explicit images in chats, the site checks images inside your own browser using an open-source model. Images are not sent to us for this check, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local and session storage to remember your theme, settings, UI sound choice, chat preferences and whether you have already seen the intro. Our sign-in provider also keeps a session in your browser so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising cookies.

Clearing your browser data signs you out and resets the settings saved on that device. If you are logged in, most settings come back from your account.

How long we keep things

We keep your account and content while your account is active. Moderation records and backups are kept for add how long. If you ask us to delete your account we will remove or anonymise your personal information, except where we need to keep a record to enforce a ban or follow the law.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and delete your own content at any time.
  • Use Settings to export your settings as a file, reset them, or change what the site shows.
  • Mute, block and report people in chats.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.

To make a request, Reach us through our Discord server, the Contact Us page, or add a contact email.

Children

SPECSOFT® is not meant for children under 13, confirm age. If we learn that someone under that age has an account, we will remove it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as using access rules in our database so people can only change their own data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and never share it.

Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in other countries than yours. By using the site you understand your information may be transferred there.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will tell you about big changes inside the site.

Contact

Reach us through our Discord server, the Contact Us page, or add a contact email.
