Why am I seeing this?

SPECSOFT Chats covers messages that look like swearing or harsh language with a warning, so you choose whether to read them.

  • It only happens on your screen. The sender is not told, and their message is not changed.
  • It works from a word list, so it can miss things or flag a harmless message. Show message always works.
  • You can block or report the sender straight from the warning.
  • Images are checked on your own device by a small AI model, and nothing is uploaded for the check. It cannot spot graphic violence, so report anything harmful you see.
